# Badge Breaker level references

An independent implementation inspired by Glenn Nissen's 1994 Windows **Ballistic**, not the unrelated 1999 puzzle game. Gameplay reference: https://www.classicdosgames.com/game/Ballistic.html

The ten implemented pickup effects are wide paddle, extra life, catch, lasers, three-ball, eight-ball, small paddle with triple scoring, Mega Ball, penetrating Blue Ball and regenerating Two Ball. Timings, physics and level difficulty are newly implemented rather than claimed to match the original executable exactly. No original game code, graphics or audio have been copied.

## Level imagery

The 20 club names and crest URLs were retrieved from ESPN's English Premier League team feed on 2 September 2026: https://site.api.espn.com/apis/site/v2/sports/soccer/eng.1/teams

Flag references: https://flagcdn.com/ . The specific image URL for every crest and flag is recorded in `../ballistic-levels.json` under `source`. Downloaded PNG references are stored unchanged in this directory. Level 1 uses Manchester United's actual crest, translated into a 32 × 32 coloured brick layout. The supplied Ballistic screenshot informed the beveled-brick presentation, not the level content.

Club names and crests belong to their respective owners. Availability through a CDN does not grant a commercial-use licence. The game displays an independent/unaffiliated notice; that notice is not a substitute for any permission needed for publication or commercial distribution.

The level data is generated by colour-sampling the images into brick cells, with transparent cells omitted. It contains 20 unique crest layouts and 30 unique flag layouts. All game progress is stored separately under `jd-badge-breaker-v1`.
